Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy explains how ServeLinkAI collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website or interact with our automation systems.

Note for Site Administrator: Text marked with highlighted tags like [Insert Registered Company Name] represents editable placeholders that should be updated with your official registered company details upon legal incorporation.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit the ServeLinkAI website (www.servelinkai.com) or submit an inquiry, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Details: Your name, business name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, and website URL submitted via our contact or qualification forms.
  • Service & Project Requirements: Details regarding your trade industry, current lead handling challenges, and preferred automation setup.
  • Technical Usage Data: Standard internet log data including anonymized IP address, browser type, referring pages, and device information to maintain site security and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process the information collected strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Evaluating your trade requirements and providing custom proposals for AI automation setup.
  • Communicating with you regarding demonstrations, project timelines, and technical support.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of our website and client lead storage systems.
  • Fulfilling legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Data Handling in AI Receptionist Systems

For clients utilizing our AI Website Receptionist solutions, end-user inquiries submitted by homeowners or property managers are captured to qualify the requested trade service and forwarded to the contractor.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize end-user conversation records or contact information to any third-party marketing brokers. Data processed on behalf of clients is stored in secured database environments utilizing Row Level Security (RLS) and strict access controls.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use trusted technical infrastructure partners to host our website, manage databases, and route communications. These providers process data strictly on our instructions and in compliance with security standards:

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages / Cloudflare for fast, static delivery.
  • Database & Storage: Supabase (PostgreSQL with RLS) for encrypted lead records.
  • Automation: Make.com for secure webhook routing when activated by the client.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal, essential functional cookies required for core navigation and form submission. We do not deploy intrusive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain contact inquiries only as long as necessary to fulfill the initial inquiry, carry out ongoing agreements, or satisfy statutory record-keeping requirements. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under UK/EU GDPR or similar data protection frameworks), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your information when no longer required for legitimate business purposes.
  • Withdraw consent to future communications at any time.
