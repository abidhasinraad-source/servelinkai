Plumbing & Drainage

Plumbing inquiries range from catastrophic burst pipes to routine tap replacements. ServeLinkAI triages active leaks immediately and secures emergency callouts.

Standard Qualification Criteria:
  • • Is water actively leaking or isolated at the stopcock?
  • • Exact postcode to confirm engineer route
  • • Emergency callout rate acknowledgement
  • • Photos or video upload instructions

Electrical Contractors

Filter out tire-kickers for consumer unit replacements, rewires, and EV charger installs while instantly escalating total power outages and burning odors.

Standard Qualification Criteria:
  • • Safety check: Any sparking, burning smell, or exposed wires?
  • • Is whole property without power or single circuit tripped?
  • • Residential vs commercial tenancy setup
  • • EV charger distance to primary consumer unit

HVAC & Heating Engineers

Peak heating breakdowns in winter and AC failures in summer generate massive spikes in website traffic. ServeLinkAI captures every job while your engineers are on the road.

Standard Qualification Criteria:
  • • Boiler / Heat Pump / AC unit brand and display error code
  • • Total loss of heat in freezing temperatures (priority triage)
  • • Property occupancy notes (elderly, infants, commercial)
  • • System age and service history

Roofing Contractors

Qualify storm damage claims, flat roof replacements, gutter repairs, and chimney repointing without having to visit unviable or out-of-budget jobs.

Standard Qualification Criteria:
  • • Property height (single storey, 2-storey, 3+ storeys for scaffolding)
  • • Is rainwater actively entering the living space?
  • • Approximate age and roof type (slate, tile, felt, rubber)
  • • Photos of internal damp or external damage

Garage Door Services

Homeowners stuck with a car trapped inside or a door dangling off its tracks require immediate help. ServeLinkAI qualifies door dimensions, motor brands, and urgency.

Standard Qualification Criteria:
  • • Security risk: Is door stuck wide open or closed shut?
  • • Door style: Roller shutter, sectional, or up-and-over
  • • Snapped cable, bent track, or faulty motor mechanism
  • • Vehicle trapped inside (urgent dispatch trigger)
